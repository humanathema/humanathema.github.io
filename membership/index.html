More than one service? Take 15% off the lot.

A household on two or more service tiers pays the combined price minus about 15% — one payment, one point of contact. For example, standard Grounds ($35*) plus weekly tutoring ($160*) works out around $165/month instead of $195.

The Kaha Tahi Community Fund

Kaha Tahi Membership is a membership programme — like belonging to a club or a co-op — run by Kaha Tahi Ltd. Alongside it sits the Community Fund: a ring-fenced pot inside the company's accounts, funded by the part of contributions left after programme costs, by any trading surplus we choose to put in, and by grants and koha.

  • $100 joining contribution — payable up front, or built up at $5–$10 a week with full membership from day one.
  • $5 / month ongoing (or $50/year) — waived while you hold a paid service membership.
  • One vote, the same as every other member, regardless of how much you've put in.
  • You stand for and elect the Members' Advisory Committee, which reviews the Fund's accounts and recommends what it backs.
  • The Fund provides grants and hardship assistance, pays for shared tools, training and our housing-first work, and can invest in members' own work and enterprises — with the membership voting on anything significant.
  • The Fund only ever does business with members.
Your contribution is not an investment product. It earns no interest and carries no promised return, and you have no right to get it back. If you leave, you can ask for it back — the directors may return some, all or none, at their discretion and depending on what the Fund can afford. It's a members-only mutual fund, not a bank or a savings scheme, and we don't take a cut for admin. We're building toward a member-owned credit union or community trust — that's when regulated saving and investment begin.
