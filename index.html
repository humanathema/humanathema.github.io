One membership

Small, predictable payments — not a bill you can't see coming

The people we're for are the ones who get the worst deal: can't do the work themselves, can't fit a one-off tradesperson or mechanic into the budget, so the car, the house, the section quietly get worse until it's a crisis.

A membership fixes that. You pay a small amount each month, we keep on top of the work on a regular cycle, and we show you how to do the simple things yourself. One membership covers whichever services you need.

In detail

What each service covers

Each of these runs for our own crew and community first, then we make it available more widely — at the same standard.

Education & training

Patient, structured tutoring built around the learner

One-on-one and small-group tuition for local learners of all ages, with maths support for secondary and undergraduate. A weekly membership locks in a regular slot with the same tutor; casual sessions are there when you need them. Sliding scale — nobody's turned away for cost.

  • Weekly locked slot, same tutor, progress notes each term
  • Casual sessions by arrangement
  • Flexible around school terms
  • Sliding scale for families who need it
Tutoring membership

For homes, sites & business

Trained crews and reliable hands, when you need them

We staff our own property and maintenance jobs from the same pool of vetted, trained people we make available for hire — so you get people we'd put on our own sites. Charged hourly, with a retainer rate for regular ongoing crews.

  • Vetted, trained, reliable crews
  • Short or long-term placements
  • General labouring, site work, moves and clean-ups
  • Local labour for local jobs

Automotive

Keep your car alive — and learn how to do it yourself

For people who can't do their own car maintenance and can't afford a mechanic to do it. A small monthly membership covers oil changes, seasonal safety checks, and getting your WOF sorted — and we'll teach you the basics on your own car so you need us less over time.

  • Regular oil & filter changes, done on schedule
  • Seasonal safety checks — tyres, brakes, lights, fluids, battery
  • WOF pre-check and we take it in for you
  • Hands-on sessions so you can do the simple jobs yourself
Automotive membership

Property & grounds

Lawns, hedges and upkeep, on a regular cycle

Repairs and upkeep for homes and small sites, done by crews you can trust — the way we'd want our own place looked after. Regular membership keeps it from getting away on you.

  • Lawns and edges on a fortnightly / monthly cycle
  • Hedges, shrubs and green-waste removal
  • Gutters, minor repairs and an annual property check on the higher tier
  • One-off jobs too, member or not

Why we're a social enterprise

We're a business, but not one built to make a few people rich. The point is to lower what things cost for the people paying, lift what the people doing the work get paid, and put whatever's left back into the community.
